Assumptions

  • All amounts are take-home money. Nothing is taxed or rounded except to the cent.
  • Projection = actual weeks where you entered them, expected income for every other week.
  • Recovery is only spread across weeks that are still ahead of you. Past weeks left blank are assumed to have paid your expected amount.
  • Savings is taken out of what's left, but it is not counted as an expense.
  • Your budget is saved in this browser only. Weekly actuals clear when a new month starts.
